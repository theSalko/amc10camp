Video Feedback Privacy and Permission

This notice covers fresh, voluntary feedback submitted through our camp feedback Google Form by a parent or legal guardian. It explains the information we collect and the separate permission for publishing video or written testimonials.

Your choice. Participation is optional and does not affect camp enrollment. Private feedback stays private. We publish selected video or written feedback only with affirmative parent or guardian permission, as chosen in the form, on amc10camp.com only.

You can withdraw publication permission at any time by emailing shefscholars@gmail.com.

1. Who handles your information

Shef Education LLC, trading as Shef Scholars, is responsible for this feedback. Contact shefscholars@gmail.com for privacy questions, requests, or withdrawal. The form is for parents and legal guardians to complete, rather than children submitting on their own.

2. What we collect and why

  • Your name and email address, and the student's first name, to identify the family and contact you about the submission.
  • The video you submit, including any image, voice, names, or other details it contains, to review your feedback and, with permission, publish a testimonial.
  • Optional written feedback, for internal review and improving the camp, and for website publication only if you explicitly select that permission.
  • Your reward selection, to administer the offer described in the form.
  • Your attribution and permission choices, submission time, and a record of the consent wording, to document and respect your choices.

Publication relies on your consent. We use information needed to arrange your requested reward to perform that agreement, and retain limited consent and withdrawal records to demonstrate and respect your choices. Publication permission does not authorize publishing your email address, private written feedback, or other form responses.

3. Separate publication permission

Reading this page or submitting private feedback does not give publication permission. In the form, you may authorize both your uploaded video and written feedback, only your written feedback, or keep everything private. If you choose publication, you grant Shef Education LLC non-exclusive permission to publish only the content types you selected as testimonials on amc10camp.com only. For an authorized video, this includes its image and voice. These limits apply:

  • You keep ownership. We may trim the video and add captions without changing its meaning.
  • We use only the public display name you choose. Choosing no displayed name does not make a visible face or recognizable voice anonymous.
  • This permission does not cover social media, paid advertisements, other websites, or unrelated uses. Those would need separate permission.
  • Please submit only material you have authority to share and authorize, including permission for your child where applicable.

Honest positive or negative feedback qualifies under the offer in the form. Receiving the offered reward does not depend on whether we publish your submission. When we publish an incentivized testimonial, we clearly disclose the incentive alongside it, regardless of sentiment.

4. Storage, access, and public visibility

We use Google Forms and Google Drive to collect and store responses and uploaded videos. We restrict staff access to people handling feedback, rewards, and authorized publication. Google processes information to provide these services under its Privacy Policy. Our website host processes content needed to serve any published testimonial.

Source files and form responses are not made publicly accessible as a submission folder or response list. Selected video or written feedback covered by your permission can be viewed publicly on our website, including by visitors outside your country. Our team works internationally, and Google and our host may process information in other countries.

5. Retention and withdrawal

We keep videos and written feedback while needed for the authorized feedback and publication purposes, unless you withdraw permission sooner. We delete them when no longer needed. Limited reward, consent, and withdrawal records may be kept where needed to administer the offer, honor your choices, or meet applicable legal obligations.

Email shefscholars@gmail.com to withdraw permission or ask to access, correct, or delete your information. We will stop future testimonial use and remove the affected content from our website following withdrawal. We cannot recover copies already saved by visitors or independently cached by third parties. Withdrawal does not affect your camp enrollment.

6. Relationship to camp policies

These specific privacy and permission terms govern this voluntary feedback form, including optional testimonial publication, wherever the general Camp Privacy Policy or Camp Terms differ. The general Submitted Content license does not apply to these submissions; this permission is not perpetual or irrevocable. Private class recordings remain separate and are not authorized for public or marketing use by this form.

The Camp Privacy Policy provides further information about your privacy rights and our contact details. Any new publication purpose requires separate permission; updating this page does not expand permission already given.
