Why most preparation stalls

Knowing the topic is not the same as finishing the problem.

Most students who plateau around 70 or 80 aren't missing theory. They're missing the move that turns a familiar setup into a finished answer in ninety seconds.

This program teaches the theory in four dedicated tracks, then spends every remaining hour on problems, under time, with feedback on what you actually wrote.

The structure

Four tracks, running in parallel.

Every AMC 10 is built from the same four areas. So is this camp. You work all four every week instead of finishing one and forgetting it by November.

Track 01

Algebra

Factoring and symmetric expressions, telescoping and systems, inequalities and AM-GM, sequences, polynomials and Vieta's formulas, and statistics, building to a full theory review before the bootcamp.

Track 02

Geometry

Angle chasing, similarity and congruency, coordinate geometry, area techniques, circle geometry, and 3D geometry, building to a full theory review before the bootcamp.

Track 03

Combinatorics

Smart casework, probability, restrictions and complementary counting, logic puzzles, stars and bars, PIE, and recursive counting, building to a full theory review before the bootcamp.

Track 04

Number Theory

Modular arithmetic, prime factorization, divisibility, digit problems, Diophantine equations and bounding, and factorial number theory, building to a full theory review before the bootcamp.
