One hundred is seventeen questions. Not twenty-five.

The AMC 10 gives 6 points for a correct answer, 1.5 points for a question left blank, and nothing for a wrong one. Sixteen right with three left blank clears 100. So does seventeen right. Either way, eight questions on that paper are not your problem.

6points, correct answer
1.5points, left blank
0points, wrong answer

Most students sitting in the 60s and 70s are not missing theory. They are spending eleven minutes on question 22 and never reaching question 14. This program works on both halves: the theory that earns the seventeen, and the judgment about where the other fifty-eight minutes go.

Before you pay

Who this is for, and who it isn't.

I would rather you read this and close the tab than enroll into the wrong room. If you are between the two columns, book a call and I will tell you honestly.

This is built for your child if

  • They have scored roughly 40 to 90 on a recent AMC 10, real or practice.
  • Or they have never sat the AMC 10, but are comfortable with Algebra 1 and basic geometry.
  • They finished our summer Basics camp and want the rest of the paper, not just problems 1 to 8.
  • They are in grade 10 or below and under 17.5 years old on competition day, which is what the MAA requires to sit the AMC 10.
  • They can attend two live classes each Sunday.

This is the wrong room if

  • They are already scoring above 110. You would be paying me to move too slowly. Email me and I will point you somewhere better.
  • They have not finished pre-algebra. The classes assume fractions, negatives, and basic exponents already work.
  • You want a guaranteed number. Nobody can sell you that, and I say so plainly further down this page.
  • Weekends are already full. The lectures are Sunday mornings, and recordings are a backup, not a plan.
  • Camera-on is not something you are comfortable with. This is taught as a live conversation, not a broadcast, and every session is recorded and shared with the other enrolled families.

Questions

When does it actually start?
Sunday, December 6, 2026. Classes are on December 6, 13, 20, and 27, from 11:00 a.m. to noon and 12:30 to 1:30 p.m. ET.
How much work is it outside class?
You get out what you put in. Two hours a week of live class, plus time to practise on your own. The students who make the most of the classes are the ones who work on problems between them.
What's the difference between this and the Basics camp?
The summer Basics camp covered AMC 10 problems 1 to 8. This one is built for the whole paper and a score above 100, now with a monthly pass and two Sunday classes each week.
My child didn't do the summer camp. Can they still join?
Yes. You do not need to have attended a previous Shef Scholars camp. If you are unsure whether this is the right level, book a call and I will tell you honestly.
What if my child can't attend every session live?
Every session is recorded and shared privately, and recordings stay available through February 28, 2027. But recordings are a backup, not a plan. A student who watches most of this instead of attending it will get much less out of it.
My child already scores above 110. Should they join?
Probably not. This program builds theory that a 110 student already has. Email me and I will tell you honestly what I think would help more.
How do we actually register for the AMC 10?
Through your child's school, or through an authorized testing center if the school does not host it. Families cannot register with the MAA directly, and sites usually close two to three weeks before the competition. Check the registration deadline at your testing site. Enrolling in this program does not register your child for the exam. If you are stuck, book a call and I will help you find a site.
Which sessions are recorded, and is my child on camera?
All sessions are recorded, and yes. This is taught as an interactive class, not a lecture broadcast, so students join with their camera on and will appear in the recordings. Recordings are shared only with enrolled families through a private link, are never posted publicly or used in marketing, and stay available through February 28, 2027. If your child is ill or something comes up at home, email me and we will work around it for that session.
What's the refund policy?
Full refund through December 10, 2026, for all three options. If it is not the right fit, email me by December 10 ET and I will refund you in full.
Is this affiliated with the MAA or the AMC?
No. We are not affiliated with the Mathematical Association of America, and nothing here is endorsed by them. We prepare students to do their best on their competition.
Where do classes happen?
Live on Google Meet. You get the link and the calendar invites as soon as you enroll.
Do you guarantee a score of 100?
No, and be careful of anyone who does. Every student starts somewhere different and progress depends on the work they put in. What is guaranteed is the structure, the material, and my attention.
